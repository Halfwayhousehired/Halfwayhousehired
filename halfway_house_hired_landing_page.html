About Us

Halfway House Hired is a mission-driven staffing service that helps residents of halfway houses reintegrate into the workforce. We handle recruiting, compliance, payroll, and onboarding—so you can hire confidently and qualify for incentives like the Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC).

How It Works

Step 1: You tell us your hiring needs
Step 2: We match you with pre-qualified candidates
Step 3: You get support + tax credits, we manage the payroll
Step 4: Candidate transitions to your team after release
